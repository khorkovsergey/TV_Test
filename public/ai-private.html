WRITTEN EXAMPLE
Portfolio risk review — example
Concentration: 62% of market exposure sits in one sector. A drawdown of the size seen twice in the last decade would move the whole portfolio further than the stated risk tolerance allows.
Currency: 88% of capital is denominated in one currency while the stated goal is in another. Over five years that mismatch has historically moved outcomes more than security selection did.
Liquidity: two positions hold most of the value and both trade thinly. Exit cost is the risk that never appears in a return chart.
Hypothesis to revisit: "rates have peaked" — the last two prints contradict it, and the positions taken on that basis are listed in the full version.
Observations about a portfolio's structure. Not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
