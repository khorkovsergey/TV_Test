What is real here and what is not. The candles, the volume bars and the watchlist are real delayed prices from a free public source — open, high, low, close and volume per bar, not a simulation. The Copilot genuinely searches the web and shows the sources it used, with the time each was published where the source gives one. The drawing tools, the indicator library and the replay control are prototype surfaces: they demonstrate the workflow and compute nothing. No broker is connected. Delayed data is not a market-data licence, an answer built from search results is not research you should act on, and none of this is investment advice.
