One loop instead of four disconnected links

How points are earned

Rules, stated before anyone plays

Points come from things that help other people: an idea whose outcome is tracked, a lesson others finish, a referral who actually activates, a script others use. They do not come from volume.
Anti-abuse: a referral counts on activation, not on signup; an idea counts when its outcome is recorded, win or lose; self-referral and duplicate accounts void the balance. A loop that rewards noise produces noise.
What points are not: money, a security, or a promise. They buy merchandise, gift plans and programme access.
