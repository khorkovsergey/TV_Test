Click the level where the price stalled twice

Two instruments over the same period. Find the level the solid line bounced off more than once and click it on the chart.

That's the one. The solid line reached this area twice and turned away both times. A level that repeatedly halts a fall is called support — it is an observation about past behaviour, not a promise about the future.
Not quite — look for a height the solid line touched more than once without breaking through.
Notice the solid line rose more than the dashed one, but its dips are deeper too. Comparing both sides is the point of this step.
